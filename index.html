LogNow for iOS

in development

An iPhone logging utility built around simple user-defined trackers. LogNow records quick, timestamped entries and is being developed with home-screen widget support and CSV data exchange in mind.

LogNow for Android

in development

The Android counterpart to LogNow, focused on fast event logging, reusable trackers, straightforward data management, and eventual widget-based entry from the home screen.

About Sudoclet

Sudoclet is an independent, non-commercial development site. I programmed for earlier generations of computers and am now learning current development tools and platforms, with a particular interest in iOS, Android, JavaScript, and interactive web technologies.

This site is also a place to publish experiments as they become useful enough to share. Projects may change substantially as I learn, test ideas, and refine their design.
